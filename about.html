About Our Institution

Discover the legacy of Maratha Vidya Prasarak Samaj.

Our Legacy

The Maratha Vidya Prasarak Samaj is one of the most prestigious centers of learning in Maharashtra, established in 1914. KBT College of Engineering was established in 1999 to provide quality technical education.

We are affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) and approved by AICTE. Over the decades, we have transformed thousands of students into successful engineers, entrepreneurs, and researchers.

Our Vision

To be an internationally accredited, multidisciplinary, and multi-collaborative institute working on technology-enabled platforms fostering innovations and patents through state-of-the-art academic systems.

Our Mission

To educate and train undergraduate and postgraduate students in Engineering and Technology by imparting knowledge, skills, and values to serve society effectively.
