About us

Our kitchen

Godavari Thali House started as a six-table mess near Panchavati. Today we serve about 300 plates a day, but the masala is still ground in-house every morning and the bhakri is still rolled by hand.

What we cook with

Jowar and bajra from farms around Sinnar, groundnut oil, and kala masala made from our own roasted spice blend. No ready-made gravies.

Who we cook for

Families, students and office groups. Every dish on the menu is marked as vegetarian or non-vegetarian, and we can reduce the spice on request.
